ABOUT WORLDLYN

Built with curiosity.

SW

Saurabh Waghmare

Founder & Creator of WORLDLYN

WORLDLYN is an independent digital platform created to bring global developments, financial markets, business, economy, technology and emerging trends together in one place.

The idea behind WORLDLYN is to make complex developments easier to understand while giving readers a broader view of what is happening around them.

Why WORLDLYN?

News is everywhere. Context is not.

WORLDLYN focuses on the connection between world events, markets, businesses, technology and the economy.

The platform is being developed as a continuously updated digital news and market-information experience.
