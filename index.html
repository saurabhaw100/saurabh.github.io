WORLDLYN INSIGHT

Don't just follow the headline. Understand the story.

WORLDLYN is built around a simple idea: information is more useful when people understand what happened, why it happened and why it matters. The platform brings different parts of the global economy together so readers can see the bigger picture.

Founder & Creator of WORLDLYN

WORLDLYN is an independent project created by Saurabh Waghmare with the goal of bringing markets, business, technology, economics and global developments together in one accessible platform.

Why WORLDLYN?

The modern world produces enormous amounts of information every day. WORLDLYN is designed to make that information easier to discover, understand and connect.

The platform combines automatically updated news with interactive financial-market tools, allowing readers to move from a headline to the bigger picture.

WORLDLYN is also a personal learning and entrepreneurship project — bringing together interests in business analytics, markets, technology and global affairs.
