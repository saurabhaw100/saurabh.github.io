What happened?

Markets, businesses and economies are constantly influenced by developments happening around the world. From economic policy and interest rates to technology and geopolitical developments, several factors can change the outlook for investors and businesses.

Why does it matter?

Understanding the bigger picture is important because individual events rarely happen in isolation. A change in one major economy can affect companies, investors, currencies, commodities and markets around the world.

What should you watch?

Investors and businesses should pay attention to economic data, central-bank decisions, corporate announcements, technology developments and major global events.
